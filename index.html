Traditional Korean Ink-Wash Painting

물끄러미 바라본 그곳

봄볕에 흔들이는
바람같다
마음 머무는 풍경속
바람되어 그 곁애(愛) 머물다

Gazing fixedly at that place,
Like the wind swaying in the spring sun,
Within the landscape where the heart rests,
I become the wind, and dwell by your side.
